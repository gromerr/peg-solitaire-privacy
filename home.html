Peg Solitaire:
Into the Stars

One board. A sequence of thoughtful moves. Explore a space-themed take on the classic peg solitaire puzzle.

Jump a peg over another peg into an empty space to remove the jumped peg. Plan ahead, work through each board and discover new challenges.

Explore the puzzles

Discover Classic and Horizon boards with different layouts and rules.

Learn with every move

Use undo to revisit a decision and try another route through the puzzle.

Keep your progress

Your game progress is stored on your device. No publisher account is required.

Google Play Games

In builds with Google Play Games enabled, sign-in connects your player profile to in-game achievements and reports their progress to Google. You can also play without connecting to Play Games. Achievement synchronization does not back up the entire saved game.
